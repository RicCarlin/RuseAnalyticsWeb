What it does

Organised around the incident lifecycle (NIST CSF functions) and the Identity Threat Detection & Response workflow.

Intrusion chain

The whole attack as a storyline, one lane per kill-chain stage. Every sensitive change by a suspect account, or made to it, is a node. Hand-offs show an account being reset, created or elevated and then acting. A "footholds left behind" list says what to undo.

Probabilistic risk model

Learns each account's normal behaviour from your own history, then scores how unlikely recent activity is. Gives P(compromised) per account and P(domain compromise) for the environment, with confidence ranges.

Blast radius

An onion view of how many steps each account is from domain control, with Tier-0 groups and rights at the core. Shows the routes in and the single change that cuts them. Exports to CSV and printable PDF.

Identity exposure

Who holds privileged membership today and over time, which rights grants exist (including DCSync), and an impact map and attack graph of what a compromised account can reach.

Live threats

A monitoring dashboard with an alert queue and scheduled re-scoring, and a warning if Change Auditor stops delivering events.

Baseline analysis

Compares the incident window with normal and gives each change a verdict: roll back, review or routine. Also flags first-ever activity, such as change types never seen in the baseline.

Detections mapped to ATT&CK

Privileged group changes, new accounts, GPO modifications, DCSync rights, off-hours admin activity, KRBTGT resets, new services and scheduled tasks, and password spray. Each is tagged to a MITRE technique and viewable in a Navigator layer.

Ransomware match

Compares observed behaviour with known ransomware group tradecraft to suggest which family you may be dealing with.

Evidence check

Compares every claim in an AI narrative with the data it was given, so unsupported statements are caught before they reach a report.

Respond and recover

From "what happened" to an ordered plan to put the directory right.

Account triage & remediation board

Rank accounts by risk, decide what to do with each, and track actions to completion with owners and due dates.

Recovery planning

Works out the clean boundary relative to your backup point and what has to be rebuilt.

Restore a backup, then replay the legitimate changes made since, in dependency order. Suspect changes are skipped and uncertain ones go to manual review.

Mode B: DIT surgery

Offline NTDS.dit repair plans: attribute restores, object deletions, and membership and link removals, in removal order.

KRBTGT reset wizard

A guided double reset of the krbtgt account.

IR report

A written incident report generated from the case, ready for the client or the board.

A risk model you can re-derive

The model does not guess from a black box. It counts activity per account and signal in a baseline window and an incident window, then:

  1. Learns each account's normal rate from this environment's own history (Gamma-Poisson with a pooled empirical-Bayes prior).
  2. Scores how unlikely the incident-window activity is against that baseline.
  3. Rolls signals up into kill-chain stages, discounting stages that normally occur together here.
  4. Runs a seeded Monte Carlo to give each account's P(compromised) and the environment's P(domain compromise), as a mean with 5th to 95th percentile.

Authentication events add password-spray detection: a successful logon from a host that sprayed many accounts, on or after the spray day. Risk can also flow between linked accounts.

  • Reproducible The same inputs give the same seed and the same numbers, so a report can be re-derived.
  • Tamper-evident Each saved run is stored with a SHA-256 in the case's chain of custody.
  • Tunable Baseline and incident windows, prior, business hours and spray thresholds are all adjustable.

How it works

  1. Connect

    Point it at the Change Auditor SQL Server database, read-only. No agents on domain controllers.

  2. Collect

    Run the detection queries, or upload exported CSVs. Results are kept with the case.

  3. Analyse

    The risk model scores accounts. AI builds the narrative, or a rule-based fallback does when no model is available.

  4. Act

    Triage accounts, follow the recovery plan and export the IR report, with every step recorded.

Deployed as a web portal with a REST API (JSON and CSV export, Docker supported), with local sign-in or Microsoft Entra ID single sign-on.

Built for evidence and for your network

Controls that matter when the output may end up in front of a client, a regulator or a court.

Read-only source access

Needs only db_datareader on the Change Auditor database. It never writes to your audit data.

Chain of custody

An append-only, hash-chained custody log. Each entry folds in the previous hash, and one call verifies the chain and reports the first broken link. Actor identity comes from the signed-in session, never the request.

Your choice of AI, including none

Use Claude or Gemini, a local model such as LM Studio or Ollama so data stays on your network, or switch AI off and use the rule-based fallback.

Cases and access control

Cases hold evidence, analyses, notes, remediation and custody. Sign in locally or with Entra ID (OIDC with PKCE; tokens stay on the server).

Feature controls

Administrators can turn features off or restrict them to admins, including high-impact recovery tools such as DIT surgery and the KRBTGT wizard.

Usage and cost visibility

A report of AI token use and spend, so costs are never a surprise.
